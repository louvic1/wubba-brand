Privacy

This site collects one thing: what you send through the brief form.

What we collect

Your email address and your message, when you send a brief. We use them to reply to you and, if you ask for one, to prepare a proposal. We don’t add you to a mailing list, and we don’t sell or share your message.

What stays in your browser

While you write a brief, your browser keeps a draft in this tab, so a reload doesn’t lose it. The draft never leaves your device and is gone when you close the tab.

What we don’t collect

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad trackers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

Deletion and questions

To see what we hold about you or to have your message deleted, write to {{email}}. The person responsible for personal information at Wubba answers from that address.

Questions brands ask

Do you guarantee views?
No. Nobody can promise what a feed will do. You buy the concept, the assets and the rights, and the report at day 30 shows what happened.
Will people know it’s AI?
Yes. Every film we make ships labeled as AI.
Does our product look like our product?
Yes. It keeps its real shape and logo. We build the scene around it.
Can we choose the place?
Yes. If you already have one in mind, put it in the brief. If not, the concepts will suggest a few.
What if we want changes?
Two rounds of consolidated revisions are included. A change outside the approved concept is quoted as a change order.
What does it cost?
It depends on the product, the rights and the exclusivity. Send a brief and we’ll quote on a call.

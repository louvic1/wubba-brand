Constraints: no prices, no invented clients or figures, facts keep the previous site's wording, Wubba is the only public identity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Wubba's media kit, the sheet a brand manager already knows how to read, with one figure doing the talking. It refuses the dark agency landing page with a hero reel, feature cards and a logo wall.

OWN-WORLD: Pure black sheet, white type, one green (#72AC0E) that only marks the point: the logo dot, the decimal point of 98.8M, the primary action, focus. Sora 700 for figures and headlines, Figtree for reading, JetBrains Mono for data labels, sources and dates. Square corners, hairline rules that divide real data, a 12-column grid.

STORY: The visitor sees the figure, learns what Wubba builds, reads that the gamer was generated and labeled, sees exactly what a brand gets, then sends a brief with an idea already written.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with logo left, three links and the green "Send a brief" button. The brand line as H1 on two lines at left. Below it "98.8M" spans the grid, its decimal point the green dot, with a row of three data cells under it. Primary action visible without scrolling.

FORM: media kit one-sheet, position 6 of 7 on my ranked list, seed key 65e57244. Signature interaction: the decimal dot drops into the figure on load; the brief builder turns product plus place into a pre-written brief.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
